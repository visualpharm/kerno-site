We build software that makes everyday services in Argentina simpler: solar power for homes and appointments in public healthcare.

Bruno

Solar panels priced house by house. Bruno looks at the roof, estimates how much energy it will produce and shows the price per kWh before the owner pays. Installers get their own branded site.

Human Rounds

Appointments for public hospitals and health centers. Patients find a service, upload a doctor's order and get a slot; staff work from one console connected to the hospital system. Running at the Hospital de Pinamar and its health centers.
